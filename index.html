How we could work
on your song

Book one part or the whole journey. From a first idea to a finished record.

  1. Songwriting

    We turn your voice memo, lyric or melody into a finished song with a strong hook and structure.

  2. Production

    Arrangement, sounds and instruments. I play most of them myself, so your song gets a band without the band.

  3. Recording

    Vocals and instruments in my studio in Wiesbaden, or remote if you can't travel.

  4. Mixing

    Every part balanced and polished until the record sounds the way it feels. Ready for release.
